Common questions

I changed phone, is my progress lost? No. On iPhone your progress is backed up to your iCloud and comes back when you sign in with the same Apple Account.

A challenge is not for me. Use a joker to skip it. It still counts to unlock the next level.

Reminders don't ring. Check that notifications are allowed for Monday Quest in your phone settings.
