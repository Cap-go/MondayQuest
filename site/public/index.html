52 weekly challenges, one for every Monday of the year. Seven days each, ticked in ink.

One week

Each challenge lasts seven days and starts on a Monday. Finish all seven and stamp it DONE. Fifty-two of them make a year.

Its own page

Timers, a breathing circle, a step counter, a sketchbook, a compass, real wake-up alarms. Every challenge plays differently.

Yours only

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your notebook stays on your phone. Open source on GitHub.
