Monday Quest

Privacy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Monday Quest keeps your progress on your device and, on iPhone, in your own iCloud. There is no account, no server of ours storing your data, and no ads. The app does not track you.

This website, mondayquest.com, uses DataFast to count visits and which download buttons are clicked. That covers the site only, not the app.

Data on your device

Your challenges, checks, notes, photos and drawings are stored locally on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

iCloud backup

On iPhone, your progress (without photos) is backed up to your personal iCloud so you can restore it on a new phone. This copy lives in your Apple account; we cannot access it.

Health data

With your permission, the app reads steps and floors and writes mindful minutes through Apple Health or Health Connect, only to tick your challenges. This data never leaves your phone and is never shared.

Camera, microphone, motion

Used only for the challenges that need them (daily photo, dictation, step counting). Nothing is uploaded.

Location

With your permission, the app reads your approximate location (rounded to about 1 km) to work out sunrise for the "Rising sun" challenge. It is used on the device only and never sent anywhere.

Notifications and alarms

Reminders and alarms are scheduled locally on your device.

App updates

Updates are delivered by Capgo, which receives only anonymous technical information (app version, platform, device id for the update channel).
